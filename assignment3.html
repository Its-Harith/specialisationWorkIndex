Assignment 3: Bloom, the final instrument

Synopsis

Bloom is a browser-based audio instrument that replaces a countdown timer with a lotus pond. While you study, lotuses grow and open on the water and each one adds a note to a slowly building harmony. When the break starts, the same lotuses become playable: pressing one sends ripples across the water that make the other lotuses chime. It is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses the Web Audio API directly with no audio library.

Open Bloom (press Start session first; browsers only allow sound after a click).

Design scenario: Sonic Timer

The scenario asks for a timer that uses an evolving soundscape instead of a numerical countdown to show a focus session passing, with the soundscape building as the session goes on and changing to signal a break. How Bloom responds to the scenario

  • Time you can hear: each new lotus adds a sustained note, so progress is heard as a harmony growing and the music brightens as the session nears its end. You can keep track of time without looking at the screen.
  • Time you can see, without numbers: lotuses open over the session, and a sun (day) or moon (night) crosses the sky as a progress cue. During the break, the moon or sun rises and sets as the break runs out.
  • A clear transition to rest: when the study phase ends the sustained notes fade out and the same lotuses become an instrument to play, so the break feels different from the work.
  • Low pressure for the person: all notes come from a pentatonic scale, so any lotus can be played in any order and still sound pleasant, with no musical skill needed.

Core interactive features

  • Timer settings: three presets (Pomodoro 25/5 by default, Long focus 50/10, Quick demo 15/15 seconds) or custom study and break times.
  • Growing harmony (study): lotuses appear over the session. Each adds a sustained note from the scale, built from layered oscillators.
  • Playable pond (break): pressing a lotus plays its note and sends a ripple. When the ripple reaches another lotus, that lotus chimes, quieter the further the ripple has travelled. Lotuses can also be reached and played with the keyboard.
  • Mood (Bright or Mellow): changes the scale (major or minor pentatonic), the sound and the colour palette, so the mood changes during study as well as in the break.
  • Time of day (Day or Night): by day there is sunshine and butterflies; by night there is a moon, stars, fireflies and quiet crickets.

Extended technique: a filter that opens over the session

The harmony is passed through a low-pass filter whose cutoff slowly opens across the whole study phase (from about 700 Hz to 3400 Hz in Bright, and a darker 250 Hz to 900 Hz in Mellow). A slow oscillator makes the filter breathe a little, and a generated reverb gives the sound space. The result is that the same notes sound muffled and distant at the start and clearer and brighter near the end, so the person can hear how far through the session they are, not just that more notes have arrived. Design system

  • Colour: a set of design tokens (CSS custom properties) controls water, sky, pads and accent colours. Bright uses turquoise water and a golden sun; Mellow switches the tokens to soft blue and lavender. Night darkens the sky and adds a plum tint to the water.
  • Typography and layout: a single clear sans-serif font, a centred setup panel with grouped options, and a caption bar at the bottom that names the phase (Study or Break) and gives a short instruction.
  • Motion: slow, calm movement (drifting water, opening petals, one shared ripple style for every wave). Visitors who prefer reduced motion get calmer animation.
  • Responsive design: sizes are set from variables so four breakpoints only change a few values: medium (under 840px), expanded (840 to 1199px), large (1200 to 1600px) and extra-large (over 1600px).
  • Accessibility: visible keyboard focus on every control, keyboard-playable lotuses, and a live-region caption so screen readers hear phase changes. Sound never carries the only information, because the visuals show time too.
