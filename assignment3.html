The instrument

Bloom is a browser-based instrument for the Sonic Timer scenario. Instead of a numerical countdown, a lotus pond grows while you focus and the sound builds into a harmony. During the break, the pond becomes playable.

Open Bloom

Showing time through sound and growth

Lotuses appear and slowly open, and each one adds a note to a sustained harmony. A sun crosses the sky and sets into the water as the break begins, so progress can be read at a glance without numbers.

Making the break an instrument

During the break, pressing a lotus sends waves across the water. When a wave reaches another lotus, that lotus chimes, quieter the further the wave has travelled. A moon crosses the sky during the break and sets as it ends.

Personalisation

Users can set their own study and break times, with Pomodoro (25/5) selected by default. A Bright or Mellow mood changes the scale, sound and colours. A Day or Night choice adds butterflies by day, and a moon, fireflies and crickets by night.
